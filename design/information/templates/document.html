Information

The Upcoming Levels List catalogues upcoming Top 1–100 Extreme Demons projected to be verified and placed on the Demonlist, along with unrated Extreme Demons that would have qualified for a rating when they were made. It is a forecast of what the Demonlist is about to look like — not a record of what has already happened. Nothing here is official: positions are estimates made by the list staff against the criteria written further down this page, and they move as levels progress.

Section two

Frequently asked

Records
How do I submit a record?

Through the list’s Discord server. Read Acceptance of Records below first — a record that arrives without the required proof is rejected before anyone assesses it, not sent back for more.

What proof does a record need?

A complete, uncut playthrough (if your video has cuts, attach the raw footage as well), the level’s audio or your clicks, and a cheat indicator and fps/tps display where your mod menu provides them — on the version of the level this site lists.

Points and the leaderboard
How are points calculated?

From the level’s rank in All Levels and the percentage of your record. A verification is worth twice a 100% record on the same level, and completing a level that is not verified yet is worth 0.8 of a verification. A 100% on #1 is 1459 points, on #50 it is 663, and a 50% on #1 is 851 — position is worth far more than percentage.

Why did my total change when I didn’t submit anything?

Points follow a level’s current rank, and on a list of upcoming levels ranks move constantly.

Getting on the list
How does a level get on the list?

Staff select it against the Level Selection criteria below. There is no queue a creator joins; levels that pass go to the Pending List until an exact position is settled.

Acceptance of Records

Definition of a World Record

Two records are tracked for every level. The world record from 0% is the highest completion percentage achieved from the start of the level. The world record run is the longest recorded segment performed on the current version of the level, measured as the difference between the percentage a run starts at and the point of death or completion.

Requirements for proof of legitimacy

Before submitting a record, you must familiarise yourself with the list of mandatory proof of legitimacy, as well as the requirements for it, to ensure that the record you are submitting complies with them — otherwise it will be rejected immediately, before the verification stage.

  • the recording must show the attempt in full, from its starting percentage to the point of death or to 100%;
  • it may contain editing intended to conceal personal information, provided the original recording is linked in the notes field;
  • in-game source audio or clicks — either is accepted, both are strongly preferred;
  • the level must be the version listed on this site.

Acceptance of Records

Cheating and penalties

The list treats a record as a claim about what a player did, and the proof requirements exist so that claim can be checked. Where a record is found to have been obtained by prohibited means, the penalty depends on whether it was deliberate.

Unintentional violations

Where a prohibited modification was active but had no effect on the difficulty of the record, the record is rejected and the player is asked to resubmit without it. No further penalty follows a first occurrence.

Deliberate cheating

  • a submission ban, whose length depends on the severity and on previous occurrences;
  • removal of every record the player holds on this list;
  • a permanent Former cheater badge on the Discord server.

Submitting a record on behalf of another person — to evade a ban, to impersonate, or for no particular reason — bans both accounts for six months. Where two or more players share one account, each is banned for six months and the shared account is banned permanently.

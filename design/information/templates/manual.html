What this list is

A forecast of what the Demonlist is about to look like — not a record of what has already happened.

The Upcoming Levels List catalogues upcoming Top 1–100 Extreme Demons in Geometry Dash that are projected to be verified and placed on the Demonlist, along with unrated Extreme Demons that would have qualified for a rating when they were made.

Nothing here is official. Positions are estimates made by the list staff against written criteria, and they move as levels progress. Levels that are already rated are placed in strict accordance with their ranking on Pointercrate.

The three tiers

The order of levels is the same in all three. What changes is the threshold to appear at all.

  • All Levels — every level with a conceivable chance of being verified and published. A level’s rank here is what leaderboard points are calculated from.
  • Main List — levels that meet the standards required to be considered for an official rating.
  • Future List — levels very likely to be verified and published soon.

How to read a level

The colour of a name is the level’s state, from layout through decoration and verification to rated. Two percentages follow every level — how much of the decoration is done, and how far the best run has got. The badges are its position in each tier: a level can be #4 in All Levels and #2 in Future List at once.

Frequently asked

Fourteen questions, four groups. Anything longer than an answer lives in the guidelines and is linked from here.

Records
How do I submit a record?

Through the list’s Discord server. Read Acceptance of Records first — a record that arrives without the required proof is rejected before anyone assesses it, not sent back for more.

What proof does a record need?

A complete, uncut playthrough (if your video has cuts, attach the raw footage as well), the level’s audio or your clicks, and a cheat indicator and fps/tps display where your mod menu provides them. The record has to be on the version of the level this site lists.

What counts as a world record here?

Two things, tracked separately: the best completion from 0%, and the world record run — the longest single segment on the current version of the level.

Points and the leaderboard
How are points calculated?

From the level’s rank in All Levels and the percentage of your record. A verification is worth twice a 100% record on the same level; completing a level that is not verified yet is worth 0.8 of a verification. A 100% on #1 is 1459 points, on #50 it is 663, and a 50% on #1 is 851 — position is worth much more than percentage.

Why did my total change when I didn’t submit anything?

Because points are calculated from a level’s current rank, and on a list of upcoming levels ranks move constantly.

Level colouring

When Level Coloring is on, a level’s name is coloured by its state. It is the same scale as the status pill on the level’s own page, so a level reads the same everywhere.

Early deco1–29% decorated
Mid deco30–69% decorated
Late deco70–99% decorated
Deco doneDecoration finished, verification not started
Early verifyBest run 30–59%
Late verifyBest run 60–99%
VerifiedBeaten, not yet rated
